About us

A calmer way to update your home

Cosy Home Club started with a simple idea: your home should be easy to keep feeling good, without decorating turning into a chore. Instead of hours spent scrolling for the right cushion or the right vase, we do the choosing for you — one seasonal theme, five considered pieces, delivered to your door once a month.

We keep what's inside a secret until it arrives. We think a little anticipation is part of what makes a home feel cared for, and each edition follows the quiet shift of the seasons rather than whatever's trending that week.
