Ordering & the monthly cycle

How does the monthly cycle work?

Each edition opens on the 1st, orders close on the 15th, renewals lock in for the following month's edition on the 16th, and boxes ship in the first week of the month after that.

What happens if I miss the order window?

Once an edition closes on the 15th, it's closed for good — there's no late joining. Pop your email on the shop page and we'll let you know the moment the next edition opens on the 1st.

Can an edition sell out before the 15th?

Yes. Each edition has a limited number of spaces, and once they're gone that edition closes to new orders even if there's still time left before the 15th.

Pricing & billing

How much does membership cost?

£34.99 a month, covering one edition.

When am I charged?

Your first edition is charged as soon as you sign up. After that, you're automatically charged on the 16th of each month for the following month's edition, right through until you cancel.

Is there a discount for paying across several months?

Not currently. New members can use the code WELCOME10 for 10% off their first box.

Skipping, pausing & cancelling

Can I skip a month?

Not at the moment. If you'd like to miss an edition, the easiest way is to cancel before the 16th and resubscribe whenever you're ready to start again.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel any time. To stop next month's edition, cancel before the 16th — that's when renewal locks in and you'd otherwise be charged and shipped for the following month.

If I cancel after the 16th, do I get a refund on that box?

No — cancelling after the 16th means you'll still be billed and shipped for the following month's edition, since that renewal has already locked in. You won't be billed for the month after that.

Shipping & delivery

Where do you deliver?

The UK and Ireland only, for now.

When will my box arrive?

Boxes ship in the first week of the month, then make their way to you on your usual local delivery timescales.

Returns

Can I return a piece I don't like?

We don't offer returns. We'd rather hear from you instead — if you're not keen on a piece, let us know at hello@cosyhomeclub.co.uk and we'll take it on board.

What's inside

What will I get each month?

Five considered pieces built around one seasonal theme. We won't say more than that — the contents are a surprise until your box arrives.

Can you tell me anything about this month's theme before I order?

Just the mood, never the specifics. The shop page names this month's theme — the actual pieces stay a secret until delivery.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help — get in touch and we'll get back to you.
